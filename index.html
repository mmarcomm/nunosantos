What we do

Regular maintenance

Pruning, weeding, irrigation checks, clearing and taking the green waste away. Weekly, fortnightly or monthly — whatever the garden needs.

Planting

Olive, carob, citrus, lavender and grasses — plants that cope with the summer here without needing water every day.

Clearing & garden work

Overgrown ground cleared, new beds, gravel, irrigation lines and general tidying up before a season or a sale.

How we work

A small team, the same faces each visit.

We come and see the garden before quoting, so the price is based on the actual work and not a guess. After that it's straightforward — we agree what's needed, how often, and get on with it.
